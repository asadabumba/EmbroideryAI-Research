# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in technical disagreement.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Accept correction when a claim exceeds its evidence.
- Protect private, customer, and proprietary material.
- Focus review on reproducible behavior and documented scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, or publication of prohibited proprietary material is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's available private GitHub reporting or maintainer contact mechanisms. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This concise policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; a future maintainer may replace it with a versioned upstream text after the repository's license and governance decisions are finalized.
